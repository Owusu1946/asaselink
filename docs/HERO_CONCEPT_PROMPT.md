# AsaseLink Desktop Hero Concept Prompt

Copy and paste the prompt below into an AI image-generation or UI-concept tool. Generate one desktop landing-page hero only.

## Prompt

Design a high-fidelity, pixel-precise **desktop-only first viewport** for **AsaseLink**, a premium map-driven land marketplace for Ghana. Render a clean browser interface at approximately **1440 x 900 px**. Do not show a mobile phone, mobile layout, responsive alternatives, app mockups, or any second viewport. This must be one cohesive desktop landing page, not a poster, dashboard, wireframe, or generic SaaS template.

The product must be understandable at a glance: AsaseLink lets people discover verified estates, inspect actual plot boundaries, see availability, and move toward reservation with confidence. The map is the hero's signature visual and must feel like a real product surface, not a decorative background.

### Creative direction

Take inspiration from the clarity of Airbnb's discovery experience: a calm header, obvious next action, and effortless browsing. Do **not** copy Airbnb's brand, logo, pink palette, accommodation search bar, category rows, listing cards, heart icons, photography treatment, or page structure. Redesign the idea specifically for Ghanaian land discovery and give AsaseLink its own spatial, mineral, forest-and-ochre identity.

The overall mood is modern, premium, calm, trustworthy, spatially intelligent, and fast. Use editorial product-design restraint: precise alignment, generous whitespace, meaningful hierarchy, quiet borders, and very few controls. There is one memorable visual idea only: a refined estate map with one selected plot.

### Canvas and composition

Use a 1440 x 900 desktop viewport with no browser frame. The page background is mineral ivory. The hero begins below a floating navigation system and fills most of the viewport. Leave only a slim, quiet glimpse of the next section at the bottom, such as the top edge of an estate-discovery heading; do not render a full second section.

Build an asymmetric composition:

- left content region: approximately 40% of the width, aligned to a disciplined vertical axis;
- right map region: approximately 60% of the width, larger and visually richer;
- no equal two-column cards;
- no text enclosed in a card;
- no map enclosed in a heavy rounded card or nested panel.

The map should visually flow into the page, with a subtle boundary or gentle 8 px radius only if needed to define its edge. It must not look like a stock photograph placed beside marketing copy.

### Navigation: one capsule system

Place a sleek floating navigation near the top center, approximately 56 px from the top. The main nav is one compact horizontal capsule, about 650-730 px wide and 58-64 px high. It uses a solid warm-white/mineral surface, a 1 px soft-gray border, and an almost invisible shadow.

Inside the capsule, from left to right:

- AsaseLink wordmark, typographic and confident;
- `Explore`;
- `How it works`;
- `For companies`;
- a primary `Explore estates` action.

The `Explore estates` action must use the **same fully rounded capsule curvature as the outer navigation**, not a square or mildly rounded rectangle. Its height should sit comfortably inside the nav, with proportional horizontal padding, a deep forest-green fill, and the same soft capsule geometry scaled down. The inner CTA should read as part of one coherent pill system while remaining visually prominent.

To the right of the main capsule, place two detached circular controls with a 10-12 px gap. They share the same warm-white surface, border, shadow, and visual weight as the nav: approximately 48 px diameter. Use only a theme-toggle icon and an account/sign-in icon. Keep them balanced and intentional, not like a generic icon toolbar. Include a subtle visible focus ring around the account control, but do not depict interaction states as excessive decoration.

The navigation must feel sleek and airy, never oversized, multi-row, sticky-header-like, or crowded. Do not add icons beside the text links.

### Hero copy

Place this text directly on the mineral page background, left aligned. The headline is large and confident but not poster-sized:

`Find your place on the map.`

Use this exact supporting copy beneath it, limited to a comfortable 440-480 px measure:

`Explore verified estates, understand every plot, and move from discovery to reservation with confidence.`

Below the copy, place two actions:

- primary: `Explore estates`;
- secondary: `List your estate`.

The primary hero CTA should use the same capsule curvature language as the nav's inner CTA: fully rounded, deep forest green, comfortable height, clear white label. It can be slightly larger than the nav version, but it must feel like the same component family. The secondary action should be a quiet text or outlined action with a restrained 6-8 px radius, never another oversized pill competing with the primary.

Under the actions, show a low-contrast trust line with text and thin separators, not decorative icons:

`Verified land companies` | `Transparent plot status` | `Secure reservations`

Use sentence case throughout. Avoid all-caps eyebrows, fake statistics, and marketing jargon.

### Typography

Use a deliberate contemporary sans-serif with character, such as Geist Sans, SF Pro, Helvetica Neue, or Switzer. Keep typography highly legible and product-like. Headline approximately 68-78 px with controlled line height around 1.0. Supporting copy approximately 18 px with comfortable line height. Navigation and button text approximately 14-15 px. Use normal letter spacing; no condensed display type, tracked-out labels, decorative italic words, or tiny monospace metadata.

### Map: the unmistakable product surface

Show a believable aerial/cartographic view of an estate in **East Legon Hills, Accra, Ghana**. The map may blend a restrained satellite-inspired land texture with precise product overlays, but it must remain crisp and legible rather than cinematic or blurry.

Include:

- one irregular estate boundary polygon with a clear light outline;
- clearly separated internal plot polygons;
- light access roads and paths;
- subtle terrain and parcel texture that communicates real land;
- a small location label: `East Legon Hills, Accra`;
- a compact, unobtrusive legend: `Available`, `Reserved`, `Sold`;
- several available plots using quiet leaf green;
- a few reserved plots using muted amber/ochre;
- a few sold plots using subdued brick or neutral gray;
- one selected plot emphasized with a restrained ochre outline and lightly tinted fill.

Anchor one compact detail label to the selected plot with a fine connector line or location marker. The label contains:

`Plot A-104`

`650 m2`

`GHS 180,000`

`Available`

The selected state must be clear through label, outline, fill, and hierarchy, not color alone. Keep the map focused on a handful of plots. Do not show dozens of floating panels, coordinate readouts, GIS diagnostics, dense zoom controls, engineer-oriented map chrome, or thousands of markers.

### Color and material

Use solid colors, never gradients:

- mineral ivory background `#F7F6F1`;
- deep forest-green ink and primary actions `#173C32`;
- muted stone secondary text `#66716A`;
- soft mineral borders `#D9DDD6`;
- restrained ochre selected state `#B7863B`;
- quiet leaf green available state `#4F775A`.

Use crisp 1 px borders, very restrained shadows, and mostly square or gently rounded secondary surfaces. The nav capsule, detached controls, and primary CTAs are the only strongly rounded forms. No glassmorphism, neon, glossy 3D, blur, bokeh, glowing blobs, decorative orbs, or purple/blue gradients.

### Product clarity and accessibility cues

Every visible control has one clear purpose. Use readable contrast, comfortable target sizing, visible focus treatment, and status communication that combines words, outline, and fill. The design should imply a fast implementation with minimal client-side chrome: no loading spinners, no unnecessary controls, no excessive animation, and no visual noise competing with the map and primary action.

### Hard exclusions

Do not include any mobile phone or mobile screen. Do not show a second responsive composition. Do not recreate Airbnb or use its branding. Do not include Airbnb-style listing cards, room photography, guest/favorite hearts, pink accents, accommodation search fields, category carousels, fake partner logos, testimonials, fake user counts, pricing tables, sign-up forms, admin sidebars, full dashboards, or unrelated sections.

## Negative prompt

Airbnb clone, Airbnb logo, Airbnb pink, accommodation listings, hotel cards, room photography, heart icons, mobile phone mockup, mobile layout, split responsive preview, generic SaaS landing page, admin dashboard, sidebar, mega-menu, equal rounded cards, card-inside-card layout, rectangular CTA inside a pill nav, inconsistent corner radii, purple gradient, blue gradient, gradient mesh, glowing orb, bokeh, decorative sphere, neon, cyberpunk, glassmorphism, glossy 3D, stock-photo people, fake logos, fake metrics, cryptocurrency styling, dense GIS controls, coordinate readouts, technical map diagnostics, dozens of markers, tiny unreadable labels, distorted typography, all-caps tracking, excessive icons, every element pill-shaped, heavy shadows, giant poster headline, clutter, low contrast, color-only statuses, motion blur, collage, wireframe, low-fidelity mockup.

## Success test

The final desktop hero should make a viewer feel: **I can see the land, understand which plot is available, trust the process, and know exactly what to do next.**
